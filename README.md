## About Me

I am a Platform Engineer, Site Reliability Engineer (SRE), and DevSecOps professional with over 14 years of experience spanning software engineering, cloud architecture, infrastructure automation, and mission-critical systems.

Currently, I work as a Platform Engineer / SRE at Maximus, specializing in secure Kubernetes platforms, Amazon EKS, AWS GovCloud, GitOps, and automated compliance. My work focuses on building reliable, scalable, and secure cloud-native infrastructure for highly regulated environments.

My career has evolved from Java software development to enterprise cloud architecture and cloud-native platform engineering, giving me an end-to-end perspective on the software delivery lifecycle. I am passionate about solving complex engineering problems, reducing operational toil through automation, and embedding security and reliability into every stage of software delivery.

- **Platform Engineering:** Kubernetes platform lifecycle management, Amazon EKS, VMware Tanzu, OpenShift, Helm, Big Bang, and Zarf.
- **Site Reliability Engineering:** Infrastructure resilience, incident troubleshooting, observability, disaster recovery, and operational readiness.
- **DevSecOps:** Secure CI/CD, policy as code, container security, CIS benchmark validation, and automated compliance evidence.
